## Stop thresholds (proposed activation controls)

- Unexpected active client Job, wrong identity/source/diff, uncontrolled concurrent change or namespace/claim collision: stop new actions. Do not automatically suspend/delete/relabel to repair it.
- More than one unavailable Kafka dual-role member, lost controller majority, unexplained volume/identity changes or inability to restore required ISR before further promotion: incident hold. One-replica availability loss during a permitted restart is expected to affect RF2/minISR2 writes; it is not a reason to weaken durability settings.
- No acceptable A2progress within10minutes at a broker checkpoint, or whole A2budget30minutes exhausted: owner reviews current state; do not kill the process/operator or promise a now-instant rollback. Whole planned window is90minutes and requires explicit revision to extend.
- Monitoring resources above agreed envelope, no source series, invalid rule evaluation or wrong route: hold dependent claims. Five-minute stability is a proposed observation gate; it does not establish an application latency/durability SLO.

Routing gate A4 precedes live rule stages A5/A6. If the recipient is unknown, stop at scrape-only. Enabling rules can send natural alerts even when synthetic notification testing is disabled; do not promise notification-free activation or postpone routing review until afterward.

## Before any reversal

Capture actual desired/current revisions, operator operation/status, Ready/generations/quorum/ISR, affected target/rule status and created/changed resource identities. Review a **current-state-specific diff**, not a blind Git reset. Retain Kafka/broker/evidence PVCs and all seven-day copies; monitoring PVCs/metric history have their own selected retention. Validate persistent-claim ownership/retention effects before UWM disablement. Preserve manual sync/no prune and explain if any supported operator-driven component removal is part of a later specifically approved action.

Recovery of lost persistent data, security revocation or unsupported version downgrade is outside this rollback plan; use the separately selected recovery/identity procedure and inherited persistence contract. No success is declared until the exact affected claim's state/evidence is restored or explicitly remains OPEN. This task tested none of these runtime paths.
